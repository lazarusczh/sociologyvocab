| # | 术语 | 官方英文释义 | 中文释义 |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Capitalism | The economic system of most countries today; based on private ownership of the means of production. | **资本主义**：当今大多数国家的经济制度，以生产资料的私人所有制为基础。 |
| 3 | Caste | A closed stratification system in which a group’s status is fixed at birth and cannot be changed. | **种姓制度**：一种封闭的分层制度，群体的地位在出生时即被固定、无法改变。 |
